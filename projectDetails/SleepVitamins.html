Summary

For this design project, I was tasked with creating a packaging solution for a product of my choice. This was for my Text class, where I learned about graphic design, typography, and layout within digital and analogue media. The goal was to develop a visually appealing and functional package that would stand out on a shelf and effectively communicate the product's benefits. In particular, I focused on an engaging color scheme and typography that highlights the most important information. The packaging was designed in Adobe Illustrator and 3D mockups were made in Adobe Substance 3D Stager.

Label Layout

The label wraps around the bottle, so I divided it into panels that each have a clear job. The front panel is centered on the bottle and holds the star logo, the product name, the flavor, and a "60 Gummies" count tab at the top. Turning the bottle reveals the back, with the suggested use, the Supplement Facts table, contact information, a barcode, and certification icons. A side panel holds the tagline, "Cosmic calm in every chew." I matched the Supplement Facts table closely to a real nutrition label, using the familiar heavy rules and right-aligned percentages, so the joke is recognizable at a glance.

Typography

The product name is set in a heavy, rounded serif that feels soft and friendly, which suits a bedtime gummy. A clean sans serif handles the descriptions, flavor, and contact details for readability. The Supplement Facts panel switches to bold condensed type in black and white to imitate a real label, so it stands apart from the rest of the playful design.

Color & Illustration

The palette moves from a deep night blue (#231BBA) through purple (#A248FF) to a soft pink (#FFBDFF), so the label shifts from night sky at the top to dreamy clouds at the bottom. A holographic texture in the sky adds a shimmery, cosmic feel, and small sparkles and a star logo carry the theme throughout, which is a nod to stars being my favorite shape. Layered clouds give the label depth and frame the product name, while white and black are reserved for text and the facts panel to keep everything legible.

3D Mockup

To see how the label would look in real life, I wrapped it around a translucent purple bottle in Adobe Substance 3D Stager. I staged two bottles on blocks against a matching purple backdrop, one showing the front and one turned to show the back. This helped me check that the important information lands on the right side of the bottle and that the colors still read well on a curved surface.
